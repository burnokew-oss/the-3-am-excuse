One line — the excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a
transaction at 3 AM, in the wallet's own voice:

> My job is keeping your coins safe from hackers, scammers — and, statistically, you at 3 AM. Come back after sunrise.

The joke turns on the wallet's job: it protects the coins, and the
twist is that its owner at 3 AM is the threat it protects them from —
"statistically" implies the device keeps score. No battery, firmware,
or cold-storage cliché, and the line only works at 3 AM: the owner is
the danger after midnight. One tight line, no explanation of its own
joke.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure stdlib, deterministic, no network, no randomness) writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt`. Output: 116 characters, 118 bytes, 1 line,
UTF-8, within the 1–200 character gate.
